Innovators often declare you can never create a bold, disruptive idea and company by conducting user research – I couldn’t disagree more. The key is in how you conduct, and how you use, said user research.

I have conducted this workshop on for kick-starting your user research for product managers several times and gotten positive feedback. Hopefully this is helpful to you as you think about your own killer product idea!
